Activities in the Nyoongar Seasons

The Nyoongar Calender has six seasons, although we are focusing on three of them.

These are: Kambarang, Birak and Makuru

Pick a season to explore:

Kambarang

October to November

The season of wildflowers. Kambarang is a time of renewal and growth, when the land is covered in colorful blooms. The days are becoming warmer and drier

Free or cheap activities to do during Kambarang

  • Cycling around Perth: Matilda Bay, Kings Park, Coastal cycle paths.
  • Elizabeth Quay: Enjoy the waterfront, public art, and events.
  • South Perth Foreshore
  • Wanneroo Botanical Gardens
  • Kings Park walks
  • Bold Park trail walks
  • Perry Lakes skate park

Paid activities to do during Kambarang

  • Perth Zoo: Explore the diverse wildlife and exhibits.
  • Daytrip to Rottnest Island
  • Mini golf at Wembley Golf Course

Birak

December to January

The season of fire. Birak is a time of heat and dryness, where the Sea Breeze brings relief from the scorching sun. It is a time for outdoor activities and enjoying the natural beauty of the land.

Free or cheap activities to do during Birak

  • Beaches: Cottesloe Beach, Omeo Wreck, Mettam's Pool, Hillary's Boat Harbour
  • Kings Park walks
  • Caversham Wildlife Park
  • Fremantle Markets
  • Local Swimming Pools

Paid activities to do during Birak

  • Adventure World: Enjoy thrilling rides and attractions.
  • Daytrip to Rottnest Island
  • Outback Splash
  • Perth Wildlife Encounters dolphin swim (Rockingham)
  • AQWA

Makuru

June to July

The season of the cold and wet. Makuru is a time of rain and cooler temperatures, where the land is nourished and the rivers flow. It is a time for indoor activities and enjoying the warmth of home.

Free or cheap activities to do during Makuru

  • Visit the Perth Cultural Centre: Explore museums, galleries, and libraries.
  • Scarborough Markets
  • Maritime Museum: Free for kids under 15
  • Whale Watching - From the shore
  • Fremantle Markets
  • Walks in Perth Hills
  • Scarborough Beach whale playground

Paid activities to do during Makuru

  • Scitech
  • WA Museum
  • Bounce
  • Ice Skating
  • Cinema
  • Ten Pin Bowling
  • Fremantle Prison
